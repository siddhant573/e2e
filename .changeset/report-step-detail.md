Report steps record what they were given and where they acted. `argument` holds the text a fill typed, the key a press sent, the option selected, the files uploaded, or the value an assertion expected, redacted and capped at 1024 bytes; a secret is `<secret:name>` and anything typed into a secure field is `<withheld>`. `target` holds the box of the node a step resolved to, in CSS pixels, and the point of a positioned tap; an agent step records its last action's.
